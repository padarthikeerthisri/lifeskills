# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

Grit means working hard and not giving up on long-term goals.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

A growth mindset means believing we can improve through effort and learning.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

Internal locus of control means believing our actions affect our results.

**Key point:** Focus on what we can control and improve.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

- Believe you can improve.
- Question negative thoughts.
- Make a learning plan.
- Accept struggles.

## 5. My Ideas to Build a Growth Mindset

- Practice coding daily.
- Learn from mistakes.
- Ask for feedback.
- Set small goals.
- Keep learning new things.
- Stay patient and keep trying.

### Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

Feynman technique is a learning approach wherein we first understand a topic and then explain it to others in simple words.

### Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Focused Mode:** It is when we focus on a particular topic and think about it. For instance, solving a Java programming problem.

**Diffuse Mode:** It is when our mind is relaxed and free to think. It helps us to think in different dimensions and make connections. For example, walking after studying a difficult topic.
